# Vehicle Rental Website Research

Sprint 1 research summary for Hyrox Rental.

## Sites reviewed

- Turo: peer-to-peer marketplace with browse-by-location/date, category filters, host communication, license upload, trip check-in/check-out, and app notifications.
- Enterprise Rent-A-Car: reservation-led flow focused on pickup location, dates, vehicle availability, branch network, and clean/well-maintained vehicle messaging.
- Hertz: booking flow centered on pickup/drop-off location, dates, vehicle class, payment state, offers, and one-way rental options.

## UI and feature takeaways

- Put location and rental dates at the start of the search flow.
- Show vehicle cards with price, location, class/type, rating/review proof, and a clear detail action.
- Re-check availability before booking submission, not only during browsing.
- Keep account dashboards role-based: renters track trips/payments, companies manage fleet/bookings, admins monitor platform health.
- Use notifications for booking confirmations, payment state, and trip reminders.
- Require identity or license documents for self-drive rentals.
- Provide visible cancellation/payment retry paths so users do not get stuck after failed payment or changed plans.
